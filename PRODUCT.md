# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Content Creators & Video Editors translating video subtitles into Thai and other languages in a fast, efficient workflow.

## Product Purpose
An intelligent, fast, and resilient web-based video translation studio powered by Gemini AI. Extracts audio, auto-chunks, transcribes, translates to Thai, and allows editing, waveform visualization, and hardsub export.

## Brand Personality
Minimalist, Clean, High-Contrast Utilitarian Tool. Focused, functional, precise, zero-fluff.

## Anti-references
SaaS cliché templates with generic rounded cards, low-contrast gray text on tinted backgrounds, noisy decorative gradients, and slow multi-step wizard bloat.

## Design Principles
1. **Utility First**: Information density, high contrast, and instant visual feedback take priority over decorative noise.
2. **Precision & Visibility**: Clear timing controls, waveform audio sync, and unmistakable state indicators for AI processing.
3. **Resilience**: Clear error messaging, easy retries, and transparent cache status without hiding technical progress.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
High-contrast text (≥4.5:1), clear keyboard navigation for video/audio playback, readable typography, and explicit status aria-labels.
